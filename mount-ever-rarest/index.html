a game of rare answers, played at altitude

Mount Ever‑Rarest.

You are a snow leopard, the ghost of the mountains, and the only way up is to say what nobody else would. Right answers climb. Rare right answers fly.

The climb

How the climb works

  • Seven prompts a round, twenty seconds each, one answer apiece.
  • Common answers creep you up the trail. Answers almost nobody gives carry you a hundred metres at a time.
  • A wrong answer, a blank or a timeout earns nothing, but costs nothing either.
  • Base camp sits at 5,364 metres. The summit is 8,849. Play as many rounds as the mountain takes.
  • 🥾 the tourist trail 10 m
  • ⛰️ a quieter ridge 30 m
  • 🐐 a goat track 60 m
  • 🐆 ghost of the mountains 100 m
