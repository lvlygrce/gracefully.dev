a guessing game for a song of ice and fire

asoifle.

One character a day, drawn from the 125 people who appear in three or more episodes. Every guess tells you six things. Eight guesses, then the raven comes.

How it works

  • ✓ the guess matches the answer exactly.
  • ~ close, but not it — the same part of the world, or a season or two out, or within five episodes.
  • ✗ nothing in common.
  • ↑ the answer is higher than your guess; ↓ lower.
  • ? under the guess bar, a count of how many of the 125 still fit everything you have been told.
  • ” from the fourth guess you may ask for a hint: a line they said, or their initials if they were not much of a talker.

House is the house they were born or married into; failing that, the order, people or trade they belong to. Origin is where they were born when the show tells us — so Catelyn is of the Riverlands and Daenerys, born on Dragonstone, is of the Crownlands. Status is where they stand when the last episode ends.
